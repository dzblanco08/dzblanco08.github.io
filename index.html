I start with questions.

When a team asks me for AI training, these are the first things we talk through together.

  • What problem are we solving?
  • Should this even be an agent?
  • Where does it fit into real work?
  • Will anyone actually use it?

Training alone rarely changes habits, so I help build the support around it. Then AI becomes part of the normal day.
